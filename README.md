Real Estate Decision Support Application Using Open Government Data

A data-driven real estate decision support application integrating public data through the City of Chicago Data Portal API to support property evaluation, neighborhood comparison and informed rental and investment decisions.

<br>

🚀 Explore the Application: https://chica-go-sight.lovable.app

📌 Project Overview

Developed the concept and functional design of a real estate decision support application leveraging API-accessed open government data.

The application utilizes neighborhood-level public datasets from the City of Chicago Data Portal, including crime rates, public infrastructure, transportation accessibility, educational facilities and socioeconomic indicators, to enable data-driven property evaluation and neighborhood comparison.

🎯 Key Objectives & Functionalities
API-Based Data Integration: Retrieval of publicly available government datasets through the City of Chicago Data Portal API.
Comparative Analytics: Evaluation and comparison of neighborhoods based on multiple socioeconomic and location-related indicators.
Decision Support: Conceptualization of scoring mechanisms to facilitate informed property rental and investment decisions.
Data Transparency: Exploration of how public data can reduce information asymmetry in the real estate market.
💡 Business & Technology Perspective

The project explores how API-driven data integration, open data and digital governance can contribute to innovative, citizen-centric digital services and support data-driven decision-making in the real estate sector.

API Integration: Connection to the City of Chicago Data Portal to retrieve available public datasets.
Data-Driven Functionality: Utilization of neighborhood-level data to support comparative analysis and property-related decision-making.
